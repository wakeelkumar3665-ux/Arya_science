5 Amazing Facts About Space

Discover some incredible facts about our universe.

1. The Universe Is Expanding

Galaxies, on very large scales, are moving farther apart as space itself expands.

2. A Day on Venus Is Very Long

Venus takes longer to rotate once on its axis than it takes to orbit the Sun.

3. Space Is Not Completely Empty

Space contains gas, dust, radiation and many other forms of matter and energy.

4. There Are Billions of Galaxies

The observable universe contains an enormous number of galaxies, each with many stars.

5. We See the Sun as It Was

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth, so we see the Sun as it was in the past.
